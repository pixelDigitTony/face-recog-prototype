## Physical acceptance boundary

The tool lets an operator collect real trials with target cameras and authorized participants/presentations. Actual lighting, appearance, facial mobility, group throughput and stationary/moved/tilted/bent print, display photo, cutout, replay and camera-motion outcomes must be measured separately. A motion-check pass is neither an identity decision nor certified PAD. Repeated face segments and repeated sessions are correlated; segment counts are not counts of independent participants.
